Hello! I'm Vinay, a Senior Machine Learning Engineer currently at <b>Con Edison</b>.

I build LLM agent and retrieval systems that take repetitive, rule-based work off analysts' plates. That started with research during my <a href='https://msaii.cs.cmu.edu/'>Master's in Artificial Intelligence and Innovation</a> at <a href='https://www.cmu.edu'>Carnegie Mellon University</a> and has carried through to production systems in industry.

Things I've built:

- <b>Multi-agent analyst assistants</b> that route plain-language questions to the right analytical tools and keep context across follow-ups.
- <b>Advanced retrieval</b>: hybrid search combining keyword, metadata and vector filtering, with configurable cross-encoder or LLM rerankers.
- <b>Root-cause tooling for RAG failures</b> that traces a bad answer back to where the pipeline went wrong.
- <b>Document AI</b> that extracts structured metadata from legal contracts.
- <b>Research</b> on multimodal and multihop question answering, LLM query decomposition for hard retrieval, and conversational agents for language learning.
