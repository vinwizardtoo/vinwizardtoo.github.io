<i>Capgemini, Associate Consultant - GenAI Engineer, Aug 2024 - Dec 2024</i>

A pipeline on AWS that pulls key metadata out of legal contracts. AWS Textract reads the documents and Claude 3.5 extracts the fields; AWS Lambda and DynamoDB store and serve the results to a ReactJS web app used by the operations team, with monitoring through CloudWatch and Splunk.

<i>Tags - Document AI, Information Extraction, LLMs, AWS Textract, AWS Lambda, DynamoDB</i>
